The Operations-Heavy
Owner

Service business · 400+ transactions a year · small team

You run a service business where every job means a new quote, a new brief, a new set of moving pieces — technical specs for your team, a polished version for the client. You tried handing this to AI directly and it broke on anything past a simple request: wrong layout, missing line items, output your team couldn't actually use.

The problem was never the tool. It was treating a 400-events-a-year business like a single ChatGPT prompt. Once the work was split into the right documents for the right people, with a real system behind it instead of one long request, the result looked completely different.

Result from a real engagement: quote and briefing work now runs at roughly 85–90% automated — the owner is only needed for strategy and the genuine edge cases.
