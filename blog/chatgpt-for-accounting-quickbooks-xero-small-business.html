ChatGPT can now open your books. Start it on read-only.

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Accounting, with plugins for QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, BILL, FreshBooks and more. It drafts invoices, reconciles bank statements and prepares month-end reports. Here's what it can actually touch, why the same week's bank news matters, and how a small business should switch it on.

Short answer

On 18 November OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Accounting: its own Accounting plugin plus partner plugins for QuickBooks, Xero, Oracle NetSuite, BILL, FreshBooks, Rillet and Campfire. It helps with invoices, bank reconciliation, month-end reports, data entry and audit prep, and the coverage describes it as preparing drafts that you review and approve. What it can change depends on the plugin: several connections are read-only, QuickBooks can manage invoices, payments and customers, and Campfire can write entries with permissions. For a small business the safe start is read-only, one task (the monthly bank reconciliation), and a human who approves every entry. The same week, three US banks said losses caused by third-party AI agents with account access are the customer's problem.

One of my clients runs an electrical contracting business in Sydney. When we connected AI to his accounting system and asked it its first question, it surfaced an overdue payment nobody had flagged. No new software, no automation, just a question asked of data he already had.

That's the part of AI in accounting I trust most: reading. Finding the thing a tired person missed at 7pm. On Wednesday OpenAI made that kind of connection a standard ChatGPT feature for small businesses running QuickBooks or Xero. It can also do more than read, depending on what you allow. That's the decision this post is about.

What is ChatGPT for Accounting?

It's a bundle: an Accounting plugin built by OpenAI that gives ChatGPT guidance for accounting work, plus plugins from accounting software companies that connect ChatGPT to your actual books. OpenAI's own pages blocked automated readers, so the details below come from outlets that read OpenAI's announcement and plugin listings: TechBooky, Pasquale Pillitteri, Lapaas Voice and CellCog.

The difference between rungs is the whole story. A read-only Xero connection can find problems and draft fixes, and you make the changes. A QuickBooks connection that manages invoices and payments can make changes from the chat. Same product name, very different risk.

Can ChatGPT do my bookkeeping now?

It can do the preparation, which is most of the hours. Matching 200 bank lines to invoices, spotting a duplicate, drafting the month-end summary, chasing down the formula that broke in March. That's slow, careful work that AI does well when it can see the real records.

What it shouldn't do, at least for the first months, is make final decisions about your money. Not because the model is bad at maths, but because accounting mistakes compound quietly. A wrong category in October becomes a wrong VAT figure in January. My rule with clients is the one I use for any new employee: they draft, a person signs.

Let the AI read everything. Let it change nothing until it has earned it.

Why it matters if you run a small business

  • The boring hours shrink. Reconciliation, invoice prep and month-end drafts are exactly the admin my business-owner clients complain about most. This puts that work inside a tool many already pay for.
  • Your messy data shows up fast. With my Sydney client, building the first report exposed that most jobs had no client category. We had to write a rulebook before the numbers made sense. Expect the same: AI is very good at finding the rules your business never wrote down.
  • Your accountant's job changes, not disappears. One of my students, an accountant in Italy, is building audit software with Claude. The people who check numbers for a living are the ones best placed to use this well. If you have a bookkeeper, involve them first.
  • Banks are drawing a line. In the same week, Citi, Citizens and PNC said customers who give third-party AI agents account access are responsible for what those agents do (more below). Connecting AI to your accounting software is not the same as giving it your bank login. Keep it that way.

This fits a pattern from this month: Claude building spreadsheets, a lawsuit over AI agents that touched more than they should, and AT&T starting every agent workflow supervised. The tools are reaching deeper into the business. The winners set the permissions on purpose.

How do I set up ChatGPT for Accounting safely?

  1. Pick one task. The monthly bank reconciliation is the best first job: high effort, easy to check, and you already know what "right" looks like.
  2. Connect read-only first. If your software's plugin offers more access, decide whether you need it. Read and draft is enough to save most of the time.
  3. Write your house rules. Your chart of accounts, how you categorise common suppliers, what counts as an anomaly, your VAT or GST treatment. Put it in the workspace instructions. Without it, the AI guesses.
  4. Run last month in parallel. Ask it to reconcile a month you've already closed and compare its draft with the real result. That one test tells you more than any demo.
  5. Make the approver a person. Every suggested entry gets checked against the source record before anyone posts it. Name who that is.
  6. Keep banking separate. Don't hand any AI agent your online banking credentials. Use your accounting software's normal bank feed.
  7. Climb one rung after a month of clean drafts. Only then consider letting it manage invoices or write entries, and only for the tasks it has already done well.

Copy this prompt: your first AI bank reconciliation

Use it with ChatGPT for Accounting, or paste in a bank statement export and a ledger export in any AI tool. It produces a review list, not changes.

Read-only bank reconciliation 
You are a careful bookkeeper for a small business. You may READ our records but you must NOT create, edit, post or delete anything. Your output is a review list for a human.

Task: reconcile [MONTH YEAR] for the bank account [ACCOUNT NAME].

1. Match each bank transaction to an invoice, bill or ledger entry. Use amount, date (±5 days) and payee.
2. Produce four tables:
   A. Matched (bank line, ledger entry, confidence high/medium)
   B. In the bank but not in the ledger
   C. In the ledger but not in the bank
   D. Possible duplicates or amounts that differ
3. For every item in B, C and D: suggest the likely fix in one line and say which source document I should check.
4. List overdue customer invoices you noticed along the way.
5. End with: total matched, total unmatched, and the 3 items I should look at first.

House rules: [paste your category rules, VAT/GST treatment, suppliers you always see].
If something is unclear, write "CHECK" instead of guessing. Don't invent figures.
Before

Connect everything with full access on day one, let it "fix" the books, and find out in January which of its categories were wrong.

After

Read-only connection, one month run in parallel, a review list every month, a named approver, and more access only after a month of clean drafts.

Also today in AI

Banks: if your AI agent gets hacked, that's on you

Citi, Citizens and PNC have updated their terms so that customers who give third-party AI agents access to their accounts are responsible for what those agents do, TechCrunch reports. Citi goes further: if the tool leaks your credentials or is hacked, you're responsible for what the hackers do. Citi's own AI agents are exempt. JPMorgan and Bank of America announced similar policies last month.

What it means for you: keep AI agents away from your online banking login, personal or business. Let AI read exported statements or your accounting software's bank feed. If you bank with one of these, read the new clause once.

Google launches Gemini 4 Neon

Google released Gemini 4 Neon, a middle model between Carbon and Hydrogen, at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1 million token context. It's in the Gemini app for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, in the API and in Gemini for business. Google reports it ranks third on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and highlights finance, accounting and spreadsheet results, all vendor-reported.

How to use it: if you pay for Google AI Pro, try it on the same reconciliation prompt above, using exported files, and compare its review list with ChatGPT's. Pick the one that writes "CHECK" more honestly, not the one that sounds more confident.

FAQ

What is ChatGPT for Accounting?

It's an OpenAI launch from 18 November 2026: an Accounting plugin for ChatGPT plus partner plugins that connect to QuickBooks, Xero, Oracle NetSuite, BILL, FreshBooks, Rillet and Campfire. It helps with invoices, bank reconciliation, month-end reports, data entry and audit prep, preparing drafts that a person reviews and approves.

Does ChatGPT work with QuickBooks and Xero?

Yes. According to coverage of OpenAI's plugin listings, the QuickBooks plugin can read and manage invoices, payments, customers and payroll from the chat, while the Xero plugin is read-only. Availability depends on your ChatGPT plan, the plugin and your workspace settings.

Can ChatGPT post entries to my books on its own?

It depends on the plugin. Read-only connections such as Xero, BILL and FreshBooks can't change anything. Campfire's plugin can create, update and delete entries with permissions, with closed periods locked. The launch is described as drafting for human review, and I'd keep it that way until it has a month of clean drafts.

Is it safe to connect ChatGPT to my accounting software?

It can be, if you start read-only, give it one task, write down your house rules and have a named person approve every entry. On ChatGPT Business and Enterprise, data isn't used to train models by default. Don't give any AI agent your online banking login: several US banks now say losses from third-party agents are the customer's responsibility.

Will ChatGPT replace my accountant or bookkeeper?

It replaces hours of preparation, not judgement. Someone still has to know your tax treatment, check the source documents and sign off. The best results I see come when the bookkeeper or accountant uses AI, not when the owner uses it to bypass them.

How much does ChatGPT for Accounting cost?

OpenAI hadn't published a separate price in the coverage I read. Access depends on your ChatGPT plan and workspace settings: Business members can install it without admin approval, and Enterprise admins must approve it.
