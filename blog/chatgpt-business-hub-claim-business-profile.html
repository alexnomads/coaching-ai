ChatGPT now lets you claim your business. Here's how to do it right.

On 10 November OpenAI opened Business Hub, a beta that lets companies claim and verify a business profile inside ChatGPT, correct what it says about them and see how often they come up. Here's what it does, who can use it, and a 30-minute checklist to claim yours properly.

Short answer

OpenAI launched business profiles in ChatGPT on 10 November 2026, in beta, through a new Business Hub. Eligible businesses can claim an existing listing or create one, verify it (by phone, text or DNS, depending on the business), and update their website, description, products, prices and availability. A basic Insights view shows mentions, website clicks and the topics people ask about. Profiles are open in markets where ChatGPT operates, subject to verification; the extra "business agent" that answers customer questions is for eligible US e-commerce and online service businesses only, for now. If customers ever ask ChatGPT about your type of business, claim your profile this week and write it like a briefing, not an ad.

A week ago I wrote that ChatGPT now handles about a fifth as many searches as Google, and that most small businesses had no idea what it said about them. The most common reply I got from owners was the same question: "OK, but what can I actually do about it?"

Until yesterday, not much. You could tidy your website and hope. Now there's a front door. It's early, it's a beta and the analytics are thin, but it's the first time a business can tell ChatGPT directly who it is.

What is ChatGPT Business Hub?

It's OpenAI's new self-service area for "Businesses in ChatGPT", announced on 10 November and reported by TechCrunch, Gulf News, IT Brief and Search Engine Roundtable, whose editor claimed his own business on day one and shared the screens. TechCrunch compares it to Google Business Profiles, and that's the easiest way to think about it. OpenAI called it "an early step in bringing businesses more deeply into ChatGPT."

Two caveats worth knowing. OpenAI hasn't published its exact verification criteria, and its own tooltip says a click "doesn't confirm website visits or conversions", according to Search Engine Roundtable. Treat the Insights as a direction, not a sales report.

Does claiming a ChatGPT profile help my business show up more?

Not directly, according to OpenAI. Benji Shomair, OpenAI's VP of monetization, told reporters that joining should not change how often a company appears, as reported by Data Studios. What it changes is accuracy. When ChatGPT does mention you, it can now draw on facts you confirmed: what you sell, where, at what price, and when you're available.

That matters more than it sounds. In my coaching sessions, when owners ask ChatGPT about their own business, the problem is rarely that they're invisible. It's that the answer is vague, out of date or mixes them up with someone else. A wrong answer delivered confidently is worse than no answer.

You can't control what AI says about you. You can control what it has to work with.

Why it matters to you

  • Your customers are already asking. If ChatGPT handles a fifth as many searches as Google, some of your next customers are asking it "who's a good electrician near me" or "best events company for a corporate launch" right now.
  • It's your first feedback loop. The Insights show the topics people associate with you. That's free market research about what customers actually want to know.
  • Early beats perfect. Claiming takes minutes. Competitors who claim first get their facts in first, and an unclaimed listing can still exist without you.
  • It rewards the businesses that wrote their rules down. A clear description, a real services list and honest prices are exactly what most small businesses don't have in one place. I keep seeing this with clients: AI forces you to write down what you've always kept in your head.

How do I claim my business in ChatGPT?

  1. Ask ChatGPT about yourself first. Before you touch anything, ask it three things: "What do you know about [business name] in [city]?", "Who are the best [your service] providers in [city]?" and "How much does [your service] cost in [city]?" Save the answers. That's your before picture.
  2. Find and claim your listing. Go to Business Hub in ChatGPT, search for your business and claim it, or create a profile if nothing comes up. Use the main business number and website you want customers to use.
  3. Verify. Have your main business phone to hand for a call or text, or ask whoever manages your domain to add the DNS record. If you're on Shopify, connect the store to speed this up.
  4. Write the profile like a briefing. Say what you do, for whom, where, and roughly what it costs, in plain sentences. List real services with real names. Skip slogans: an AI can't do anything with "passion for excellence", but it can use "residential rewiring in north Barcelona, quotes within 48 hours".
  5. Make your website say the same thing. ChatGPT still reads the web. If your profile says one price and your site says another, you've created the confusion you were trying to fix.
  6. Check Insights once a month. Look at the topics. If people keep asking about something you don't mention, add it. If a topic is wrong, fix the source.

Copy this prompt: write your ChatGPT business profile

Paste this into ChatGPT or Claude with your real details. It turns what's in your head into a profile an AI can actually use, and flags the gaps before a customer finds them.

Business profile writer 
I'm claiming my business profile in ChatGPT's Business Hub. Help me write it so an AI assistant can describe my business accurately to potential customers.

My business: [name, city/area served]
What we do: [services or products, in my own words]
Who we do it for: [type of customer]
Prices: [typical price ranges or "from" prices, if I'm happy to share them]
Availability: [opening hours, lead times, booking process]
What makes us different: [facts only, e.g. certifications, years trading, specialisms]
My website: [URL]

1. Write a 3-4 sentence business description in plain language. No slogans, no superlatives, only facts a customer could check.
2. Write a clean list of services or products, each with a one-line description and price range where I gave one.
3. List the 10 questions a new customer is most likely to ask an AI about a business like mine, and mark which ones my details above don't answer yet.
4. Point out anything vague, inconsistent or likely to be misunderstood.

Ask me for anything missing before you finalise. Don't invent details.
Before

A customer asks ChatGPT about you. It guesses from an old directory listing, gets your prices wrong and recommends a competitor whose website is clearer.

After

Your verified profile says exactly what you do, where and for how much, your website agrees with it, and once a month you see what people are asking.

FAQ

What is ChatGPT Business Hub?

It's OpenAI's self-service area, launched in beta on 10 November 2026, where businesses can claim and verify a profile in ChatGPT, update their website, description, products, prices and availability, and see basic Insights such as mentions, website clicks and common topics.

How do I claim my business on ChatGPT?

Open Business Hub in ChatGPT, search for your business and claim the listing, or create a new profile. Then verify it, by a phone call or text to your main business number or by adding a DNS record to your domain, depending on the options offered for your business and market.

Is ChatGPT Business Hub available outside the US?

Business profiles are available to eligible businesses in markets where ChatGPT operates, subject to verification. Business agents, which answer customer questions on your behalf, are limited to eligible US e-commerce and online service businesses at launch. OpenAI says it will expand to more markets and industries.

Does a ChatGPT business profile improve my ranking?

OpenAI says joining should not affect how often a business appears. What it improves is accuracy: when ChatGPT mentions you, it can use facts you have confirmed. Your website still matters, so keep it consistent with your profile.

What do ChatGPT business Insights show?

Aggregated profile views, mentions, website clicks and the topics people associate with your business. You don't see individual conversations, numbers only appear above a minimum volume, and a recorded click doesn't confirm a website visit or a sale.

Does ChatGPT Business Hub cost money?

OpenAI hasn't published a price in the coverage I read, and one write-up reports it is free at launch. Business agents can also be promoted as a paid ad destination, so expect paid options to grow over time.
