Same AI. A completely different question.

The difference between a guess and real research isn't the model you use. It's what you give it and what you ask it to do.

✕ The guess
  • "Should I buy [hot stock]?"
  • An answer from the model's memory, possibly out of date
  • No source you can check
  • Confident tone, unknown accuracy
✓ The research
  • "Here's the Q3 transcript. List the 3 risks management raised, with the exact quote for each."
  • Built on the actual document you provided
  • Every point traceable to a line
  • The decision stays with you
