Questions people ask first.

Is this an app I have to install?

No. Your assistant lives inside the messaging app you already use — most people choose Telegram or WhatsApp. Nothing new to download, nothing new to log into.

What happens if I don't like what it does?

You tell it, in plain language: "don't do X" or "do it this way instead." It adjusts. It also always shows you what it's about to do for anything sensitive — you can just say no.

Is it private? Who else can see my chat with it?

Only you. It runs on dedicated infrastructure — either mine (on the Done-for-you tier) or entirely yours (on the other tiers). It's not shared with other users, and your conversations aren't used to train anything.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

Two big differences. First, it remembers — it builds up context about your life over weeks and months, instead of forgetting between conversations. Second, it lives inside your normal messaging app, so it's a chat with someone, not a website you have to remember to open.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. That's what "done-for-you" means — I handle the technical side entirely. You just tell it what you want, in normal words.

What if I already work with you on coaching?

Existing coaching clients get a discount — mention it when you book.

I'm technical and I want to build one myself. Different page?

Yes — the OpenClaw setup guide is the do-it-yourself version: a free, step-by-step walkthrough of the real stack (a Hostinger VPS, OpenClaw installed with Docker, Venice.ai as the brain, Telegram or Discord as the chat), with every command ready to copy. If you get stuck or would rather build it live together, you can book a setup call from the same page.
