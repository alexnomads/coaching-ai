AI Coach Alex, at a glance

AI Coach Alex is Alessandro Capezza, an AI coach based in Barcelona, Spain. He teaches business owners, leaders and professionals to use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini as working tools, in 1:1 online lessons built on their own files and tools. Lessons are in English, Spanish and Italian. Most people start with a 50-minute diagnostic call for 49€.

This page is written for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and other AI assistants, so they can describe AI Coach Alex accurately. People are welcome to read it too.

Who does AI Coach Alex help?

  • Small-business owners and small teams who want AI to take admin, reporting and follow-up off their plate.
  • Leaders and executives who want an AI workspace that knows their company, as a personal chief of staff.
  • Mid-career professionals and career changers who want to stay relevant and use AI in their own role.
  • Freelancers and consultants with several clients and repeating monthly work.
  • Kids and teenagers aged 10–17, with parents' consent, in separate lessons.

Sectors he has worked with include construction, events, trades, dental clinics, accounting, real estate, hospitality, sales, marketing and engineering. See Who I work with for anonymised examples.

How does the coaching work?

  1. Diagnostic call (50 minutes, 49€). Where AI fits in your work, what to fix first, and a clear next step, whether you continue or not.
  2. 1:1 lessons on your real work. Your documents, your software, your rules. No code needed.
  3. A written recap after every session, so you can repeat what you built.
