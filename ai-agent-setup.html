A chat window is not an employee.

Most people's experience of "using AI" is a browser tab that resets every conversation and lives inside a product they don't control. That's fine for a quick question. It's not an employee — it's a search bar.

A chat app
  • Forgets you the moment the tab closes
  • Lives inside someone else's product, on their terms
  • Has to be opened, typed into, remembered to check
  • Can't message you first, can't act on a schedule
  • Your data sits on whatever infrastructure they chose
A personal AI agent
  • Persistent memory — your business, your clients, your context, kept
  • Runs on infrastructure that's yours, not rented attention inside an app
  • Lives where you already message people — no new app to open
  • Sends you the morning briefing; you don't have to ask
  • You hold the keys — literally, the server login is yours

What actually gets typed, installed.

Two real commands, not a metaphor — this is genuinely what runs on the call. The Docker route is what I set up by default on a VPS; the quick install is for testing on your own machine first.

Quick install
# installs OpenClaw directly $ curl -fsSL https://openclaw.ai/install.sh | bash $ openclaw onboard
Docker — recommended for VPS
# one container, pre-configured $ docker run -d --name openclaw \ --restart unless-stopped \ -p 18789:18789 \ -v openclaw-data:/root/.openclaw \ openclaw/openclaw:latest

Questions people ask first.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

None. You need the messaging app you choose — Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp or Slack — already on your phone. I handle the server, Docker and OpenClaw setup live, on the call.

What does it actually cost to run?

Separate from my price: VPS hosting (from about $7/mo on Hostinger) and AI usage via Venice.ai, both billed directly to accounts in your own name. On the Retainer tier, hosting moves onto my server instead — zero infra cost for you.

Why Docker instead of a manual install?

Docker packages OpenClaw and everything it needs into one container — it's the difference between a consistent, easy-to-update setup and a manual install that's fragile to change. It's the method I use by default on a VPS.

Is my data private?

Yes. Unlike a chatbot or custom GPT living inside someone else's product, this runs on dedicated infrastructure — yours on Starter and Pro, mine on Retainer — not shared with anyone else's platform.

How is this different from ChatGPT or a custom GPT?

ChatGPT forgets you after every conversation. This remembers, persistently, and lives inside the app you already message people on — not a separate tab you have to remember to open.
