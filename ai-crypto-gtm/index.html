06Prediction marketsMulti-agentBase L2

Three AI agents argue before you bet.

Oracle Arena & SportSignal · Builds

I use prediction markets myself, on Polymarket, so I build for them as a user. Quests, airdrops and bounties are losing their pull. Prediction markets reward people for knowing something, which makes them a sharper engagement and intelligence tool.

What I built

  • Oracle Arena, a Venice AI hackathon project: three AI agents debate each forecast (ETH price, BTC targets, macro and AI questions) before you take a side
  • SportSignal: a dashboard that aggregates signals for Limitless sports prediction markets on Base
  • Prediction-style community challenges that reward knowledge and feed market intelligence back to the team

Why it matters for your GTM

The same debate-and-score pattern works for deciding which leads, KOLs or partners deserve your time: several agents argue, one scores, a human decides.

The AI layer I'd plug into your GTM.

A six-agent system I built for a fintech workforce platform, adapted for a protocol, wallet or exchange. Each agent does one job; all of them read and write the same context, so nothing gets lost between steps.

Signal

Watches funding rounds, governance forums, ecosystem announcements and X for moments worth acting on.

Source

Turns a signal into named teams, builders, KOLs or partners to reach.

Enrichment

Adds chain, stage, community size, past partners and the right contact.

Score

Ranks every lead against your ideal partner or customer, with the reason written down.

Outreach

Drafts a message hooked on what that person just shipped or said. A human approves it.

Coach

Reads replies and results, then tells the team what to change next week.

Shared context layer · one memory for positioning, ICP, past outreach and results

KOL scoring

Scoring criteria for creators, faster onboarding, and incentives that mix upfront fees with performance rewards, so you stop paying for one-off posts.

Community analytics

Discord, Telegram, X and YouTube in one view: community health, funnel from first touch to active member, and which content formats actually work.

Content engine

LLM-assisted scripts, editorial review and scheduled publishing. The GenLayer setup, rebuilt for your narrative.
