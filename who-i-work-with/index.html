Questions about who I work with

Owners, operators, leaders and professionals who want AI as a working tool: event companies, electricians, construction firms, clinics, accountants, CEOs, real estate agents, marketers and salespeople, plus individuals changing career. Each workshop in this city is a real, anonymised engagement.

Are these real clients?

Yes. Names, cities and identifying figures are removed; the country, or continent, is kept. Each workshop is marked Result, In progress or Getting started, so you can see where it stands.

No. Most clients start non-technical. Lessons are 50 minutes, one to one, and built around your own files, tools and daily work.
