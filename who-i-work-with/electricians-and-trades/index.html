AI for electricians and trades business owners

First winHidden overdue payment foundElectrical contractingAustralia

In shortA trades business can connect AI to its job management and accounting software to spot unpaid completed jobs, leads nobody followed up and jobs missing notes. The value is in finding leaks, not counting jobs.

The real engagement

You run a field team and an office team. Leads come in by email, web form and WhatsApp; jobs live in your job software, money in your accounting tool. You only find out something slipped when a client complains or cash is short.

One job can be worth more than ten. So the question isn't how many jobs you did. It's which money is stuck, which lead nobody called, and who on the team needs help.

First winHidden overdue payment found

On its first test the AI workspace caught an overdue payment the owner didn't know about; the next found a lead nobody had worked. A weekly real-estate jobs report went from about five minutes to one.

Questions from this workshop

Can AI connect to ServiceM8 and Xero?

Yes. In this engagement both were connected to Claude through official connectors, so reports read live job and accounting data instead of exports.

What should a trades business track with AI?

Money stuck between 'job done' and 'paid', leads not followed up, jobs with no notes, and jobs with no client type. Volume of jobs matters less.

Do I need a developer?

Not to start. Connectors and a well-written project cover the first months. Automation and APIs come later, once you know which reports you actually use.
