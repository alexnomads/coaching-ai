The real engagement

You've done real work across more than one world, and that variety is an asset. Nobody has shown you how to frame it, so it reads as scattered instead of intentional.

This isn't a CV polish. It's building an evidence-based case: market data on what the roles pay and need, and a positioning that turns your background into your edge.

Questions from this workshop

How can AI help me change careers?

Use it to research roles and salaries, map your past work to what those roles need, and prepare for interviews. It speeds up the research; you still choose the direction.

Should I make a different CV for each role?

Yes, one per role category. AI makes that quick once your full history sits in one project it can draw from.

Is this for entry-level job seekers?

No. It works best for people with real experience in more than one field who need to explain the thread that connects it.
