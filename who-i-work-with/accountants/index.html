The Accountant

AI for accountants building their own audit tools

In shortAccountants can use AI to build their own tools, such as audit checklists and anomaly checks, without being programmers. The step that matters is moving from copy-pasting prompts between chats to a structured project with a clear MVP.

The real engagement

You want to work less on routine and more on new clients. You've already built a working desktop tool for statutory audits by writing prompts in one AI and pasting them into another.

It works, but it doesn't scale. The next step is structure: one place for the logic, a defined MVP, and AI that points at anomalies while you sign the audit.

Questions from this workshop

Can accountants build software with AI without coding?

Yes, to a working tool. Structure matters more than code: a defined goal, an MVP and a project where the logic lives.

Can AI do an audit?

No. It can flag anomalies and prepare working papers. The accountant reviews and signs.

What's an MVP for an audit tool?

The smallest version that does one check end to end on real files, such as matching ledgers to bank statements.
