The Hospitality Operator Building a SaaS

AI for hospitality operators building their own software

In progressHospitality operations softwareSpain

In shortHospitality operators can build their own operations software with AI, but no-code platforms get expensive as usage grows. The usual path is to rebuild one module at a time on your own stack, then package it for other venues.

The real engagement

You run operations and accounting for several bars, and you built an app to manage them: finance, budgets, staff, legal, supplier orders. It works and people use it daily. But the no-code platform it runs on costs more every month, and the logic has grown messy.

The goal isn't just cheaper hosting. It's owning the product, so it can become software other bars pay for, bundled with the expertise behind it.

In progress

A first app already runs on its own hosting and database, outside the no-code platform. The plan: halve monthly running costs, rebuild modules one by one, and package an entry tier for other bars.

Client based in: Spain

You find yourself thinking…

  • “My no-code app works, but the bill keeps growing”
  • “I'd rebuild it if I knew where to start”
  • “Other venues want what we have”
  • “Client money data must stay private”

What you'll leave with

  • A migration plan module by module, not a risky big-bang rebuild
  • Your own hosting and database
  • A product tier other venues can buy
  • Privacy rules for client financial data

Questions from this workshop

Can I build bar management software with AI?

Yes. A working app for finance, staff and orders is realistic. Plan for ownership early: hosting, database and access rules.

Should I leave my no-code platform?

When monthly costs and messy logic outweigh speed. Move one module at a time so the bars never stop working.

How do I turn an internal tool into a SaaS?

Start with the module other venues ask about most, price an entry tier, and keep your consultancy as the premium layer.
