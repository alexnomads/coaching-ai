## 6. Voice and storytelling (Moth method)

First person, a real moment, clear stakes, something changes, honest ending (not too neat). First line pulls you in.
Show, don't tell: a scene, a number, a sentence someone said. Plain words, short sentences, no hype, no AI filler
("In today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", "unlock", "delve", "Here's the thing"), few em dashes, max one emoji per paragraph.
Never invent clients, quotes, numbers or results. If a news item is used, cite facts only from a source read that day.

## 7. Formats

**LinkedIn (Mon–Fri):** 900–1,600 characters. Hook ≤ 12 words on line 1, line 2 creates curiosity before "see more".
Short paragraphs (1–3 lines). Story → lesson → one practical takeaway → soft CTA (question to answer in comments, or
"link in first comment"). 0–3 hashtags at the end. Optionally tie to an AI/tech news item from the last 24 hours.
Deliver: post text + first-comment text (link with ?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=daily) + suggested image idea.

**Instagram (Mon, Wed, Fri):** a 6–8 slide carousel, 1080×1350, brand book (cream #f8f6f1, near-black #0d0c0a, lime #9fe870,
deep green #163300; Fraunces display with one italic lime/green phrase, Instrument Sans body; typography-led, no stock photos,
no other companies' logos, no people). Slide 1 hook, last slide CTA ("Link in bio" / "Comment SETUP"). Caption: same story,
shorter, ends with a question; 8–12 hashtags.

**Weekly blog, "From the Coaching Room" (Tuesdays):** 1,200–1,800 words. One angle from section 5, built on one story from section 4,
optionally opened with an AI/tech news item from the previous 24 hours as the hook. Practical: a framework, steps, a template or a
copy-paste prompt the reader can use today. Mid-article and end booking CTAs. FAQ. Built with `_tools/build_blog_post.py`
(tag "From the Coaching Room"); images with `_tools/render_blog_images.py`.
